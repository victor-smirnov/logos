site: src/compiler/sema_impl.hpp::check_call_outlives (B69)

THE FACT. B69 maps the callee's regions rigidly onto the caller's DECLARED regions and
demands the caller's graph satisfy the substituted pair. Rust instantiates the callee's
regions at INFERENCE VARIABLES bounded from below by the argument regions, so a bound
between two regions the caller cannot observe is satisfied at the call point and demands
nothing. The carrier that says whether the caller CAN observe a callee region is the
callee's RETURN TYPE, and check_call_outlives is never given it.
