WHY THIS BLOCK. The hypothesis the headers carry — "key the drop list by SLOT" — is one change at one registrar
(SemaImpl::define), and the two tier-1 rows are its leak and double-free directions. Reading shows it is NOT one change:
  * sema's frame record is name-keyed (Frame::var_order pushes a name once, Frame::vars overwrites) — the same-frame row;
  * but an SDrop names its binding by STRING (lir SDrop has no slot) and mlir-gen resolves it through a flat
    name -> mlir::Value map (MLIRGenImpl::scope_), so a drop emitted where the name denotes a later binding destroys
    the later binding. The param row is two sema frames (params / body) — sema's record is right there, and the
    wrong destruction is mlir-gen's resolution. Phase-1 slots are MINTED (VAR_SLOT on SLet/EVarRef, P_SLOT on params)
    and consumed by NOTHING in mlir-gen.
  * the base hand battery (30 shapes) finds the class wider than both rows: cross-frame EARLY EXITS (return / break
    out of an inner block that shadows an outer local or a param; a match arm binding shadowing an outer local with a
    return in the arm) destroy the inner binding twice and leak the outer one — no row names them.
So the block is a class whose members sit behind two doors in series (sema record, mlir-gen name resolution), and the
round's question is whether ONE carrier (the slot, carried on SDrop and resolved by mlir-gen) plus the frame record
moves both rows, and which neighbours it moves with them.
