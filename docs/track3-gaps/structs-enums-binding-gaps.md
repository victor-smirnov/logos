| B-ts-01 | Tuple-struct syntax `struct Foo(T1, T2);` | ✅ Closed (2026-05-11) — non-pub + pub forms both supported. Grammar accepts `struct Foo(T1, T2);` and `pub struct Foo(T1, T2);`; sema interns synth field names "0", "1", … in a long-lived pool (SemaFieldInfo::name is a string_view) and marks `is_tuple_struct = true` on SemaStructInfo. | B-pg-01 | peg_gen segfault: MultiChunk arena breaks RelativePtr | ✅ Closed (2026-05-11) — root cause: peg_gen used `make_doc_multi` (MultiChunk arena) for its parsed-grammar IR; once the IR grew past the initial 512KB chunk it spilled into a separate chunk whose virtual-address delta to the head chunk overflowed `arena_offset_t` (uint32_t). `TinyObjectMap::data_.get(head)` then returned a wild pointer (`base + sign-truncated delta`) and the next `put()` SIGSEGV'd inside the values array. Switched peg_gen to `make_doc` (GrowableSingleChunk) — buffer doubles on overflow within one contiguous chunk; `RelativePtr` offsets stay valid through realloc because `grow()` saves `self_off` before allocating and recomputes pointers from the new base. This was the blocker preventing the pub-tuple-struct alt from being added — it crashed peg_gen by tipping the IR just over the 512KB threshold. | Adding a single additional grammar alt could deterministically segfault peg_gen if it tipped the IR past the initial chunk. | Surfaced while wiring B-ts-01's `pub_struct_def` alt. | n/a — bug in peg_gen itself |
| S8-en-02 | Enum-discriminant const-expression (non-literal) | ✅ Closed (2026-05-11) — three shapes supported at enum-disc position: (1) integer literal `42`, (2) `metacall { <expr> }` for any ctfe-numeric value, (3) **cross-enum reference** `OtherEnum::OtherVariant (as T)?`.
